# Traffic & Growth Kit — anastanveer.com + arsdeveloper.co.uk

Honest note: organic SEO compounds over **3–6 months**. This kit is the legit, policy-safe way to (a) make sure Google can index you, (b) earn backlinks, (c) get direct clicks now, and (d) buy qualified traffic instantly with ads. Do #1 today — it's free and unblocks everything else.

## 1) Google Search Console — DO THIS FIRST (free, ~15 min per site)

Without indexing, zero organic traffic is possible. For **each** site (anastanveer.com and arsdeveloper.co.uk):

1. **Confirm the property is verified** in [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console). (anastanveer.com uses the URL-prefix + meta-tag we set earlier.)
2. **Submit sitemaps** → left menu **Sitemaps** → add and Submit:
   - `sitemap.xml`
   - (anastanveer.com also has) `sitemap-images.xml`
3. **Request indexing** of your money pages → top **URL Inspection** bar → paste each URL → **Request Indexing**:
   - anastanveer.com: `/`, `/services`, `/pricing` (or `/contact`), `/blog`, and 3–4 top `/blog/...` posts
   - arsdeveloper.co.uk: `/`, `/services` (or `/software-development`), `/pricing`, `/blog`, and 3–4 top `/blog/...` posts
4. **Check what's actually indexed** → on Google search: `site:anastanveer.com` and `site:arsdeveloper.co.uk`. Count the results.
   - Few/zero → normal for new sites; keep requesting + wait days.
   - Pages missing after 2 weeks → GSC **Pages** report shows *why* (e.g. "Discovered – not indexed" = needs more authority/links → section 2).
5. **Bing Webmaster Tools** ([bing.com/webmasters](https://www.bing.com/webmasters)) — import from GSC in one click. Bing also powers ChatGPT search → GEO win.

> Repeat "Request Indexing" for each NEW blog the day it publishes.
